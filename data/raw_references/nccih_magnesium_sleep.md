NCCIH reports that magnesium research for insomnia and other sleep disorders is sparse. Reviews found low-quality, small, or conflicting studies, so the evidence is not sufficient to determine effectiveness. This evidence concerns magnesium generally and does not establish a magnesium-glycinate-specific effect.
