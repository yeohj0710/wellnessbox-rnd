ADA defines CGM time in range as the percentage of readings and time between 70 and 180 mg/dL and lists a goal above 70% for many nonpregnant adults with type 1 or type 2 diabetes. The candidate scorer applies this only when diabetes context and a blood-glucose goal are explicit; it does not change medication, diagnose diabetes, or set an individualized treatment target.
