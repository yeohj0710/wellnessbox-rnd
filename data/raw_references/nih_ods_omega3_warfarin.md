The NIH Office of Dietary Supplements describes a possible fish-oil and warfarin interaction, notes that most research at 3–6 g/day did not significantly change anticoagulant status, and reports that FDA-approved omega-3 pharmaceutical package inserts call for periodic INR monitoring when those products are used with anticoagulants. The source does not state that dietary omega-3 products universally require exclusion.
